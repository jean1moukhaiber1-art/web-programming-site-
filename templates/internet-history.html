Introduction

The Internet is a worldwide network that connects computers and networks. It developed from early research networks such as ARPANET and later grew through technologies such as TCP/IP and DNS.

Internet Timeline

1961 : Packet Switching

Leonard Kleinrock published research about packet switching. This became an important idea for computer networks.

Source: Internet Society

1969 : ARPANET

The first ARPANET message was sent between UCLA and the Stanford Research Institute. ARPANET became an important early network.

Source: Stanford University

1973,1974 : TCP/IP

Vint Cerf and Robert Kahn developed ideas that led to TCP/IP. It allowed different networks to communicate with each other.

Source: Internet Society

1983 : TCP/IP and DNS

ARPANET adopted TCP/IP, and the Domain Name System was developed. These technologies became important parts of the Internet.

Source: Internet Society

1985,1986 : NSFNET

NSFNET connected research and educational institutions and helped the Internet grow.

Source: National Science Foundation

1990s : Internet Expansion

During the 1990s, Internet access expanded to more organizations and the general public.

Source: National Science Foundation

Key Technologies

Packet Switching

Information is divided into small packets that travel through a network.

TCP/IP

A group of protocols that allows computers and networks to communicate.

DNS

DNS connects easy-to-read domain names with Internet addresses.
